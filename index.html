What We Offer

Complete Dental Services

Advanced dental care with modern technology, cosmetic treatments, and painless procedures for every smile.

Dental Implants
Regain your confident smile with permanent, natural-looking dental implants. Ideal for replacing missing teeth, our implants provide long-lasting durability and improved chewing ability.
Aligners & Orthodontics
Straighten your teeth comfortably with clear aligners or traditional braces. We offer customized orthodontic solutions for children and adults, helping you achieve a perfectly aligned smile.
Teeth Whitening
Brighten your smile with our safe and effective teeth whitening treatments. We offer in-clinic laser whitening and take-home whitening kits to remove stains and discoloration.
Root Canal Treatment
Save your natural tooth with a painless root canal procedure. Our advanced techniques ensure comfortable, infection-free treatment to relieve tooth pain and restore oral health.
Crowns & Bridges
Restore damaged or missing teeth with high-quality dental crowns and bridges. We offer ceramic, zirconia, and metal-free options for a natural look and strong bite.
Pediatric Dentistry
Gentle and stress-free dental care for kids! We specialize in child-friendly treatments, including cavity prevention, fluoride application, and painless extractions.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Who We Are

A Clinic Built on Trust & Care

At Olive Dental Care, we are dedicated to providing exceptional dental care with a patient-centered approach. Located in Singasandra, Bangalore, our clinic offers a wide range of dental services — from general dentistry and cosmetic smile designing to advanced implantology and clear aligners. With a team of experienced dentists and state-of-the-art technology, we ensure comfortable, effective, and affordable dental treatments tailored to every patient’s needs. Our mission is not just to treat teeth, but to create confident, healthy, and beautiful smiles in a relaxing and caring environment.

Our Mission
To deliver world-class dental care through advanced technology, compassionate service, and personalized treatment plans that improve oral health and boost confidence.

Our Vision
To become Bangalore’s most trusted and innovative dental clinic by creating brighter smiles, lasting relationships, and exceptional patient experiences for every generation.

  • Cashless insurance & easy EMI payment options

  • Gentle treatment for children and anxious patients
